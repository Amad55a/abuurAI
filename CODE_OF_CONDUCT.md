# Community Code of Conduct

Somali AI Connect is a Somali-led, Somali-first developer community for people
in Somalia and around the world. We welcome contributors of every background,
experience level, gender, identity, disability status, language variety, and
place of residence.

## Our standards

We aim to make participation welcoming and constructive. Examples of behavior
that supports this include:

- Treating people with respect, patience, and generosity.
- Offering constructive feedback and accepting it in good faith.
- Making room for beginners and for different Somali language varieties.
- Discussing technical disagreements without attacking people.
- Respecting privacy, consent, attribution, and community boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
intimidation, sexualized attention without consent, doxxing, unwanted
publication of private information, and sustained disruptive conduct.

## Scope and moderation

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other project-organized community channels.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove or edit contributions that violate this code, close
or restrict participation in a thread, or take other proportionate action.
Where practical, moderation should be clear, fair, and respectful of everyone
involved.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through a
verified private contact method listed by the repository. Do not post private
information or sensitive allegations in a public issue. If the repository
does not provide a private contact method, ask for one without disclosing
details publicly.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical. We cannot promise a
particular response time or outcome, but retaliation against someone who
raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in Somali AI Connect community spaces, you agree to follow
this code. Thank you for helping build a respectful Somali developer community.
